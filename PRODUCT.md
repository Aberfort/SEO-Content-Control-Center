# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary user is an SEO operator, agency owner, or technical content lead who manages WordPress SEO work across client or owned sites. They use the product while connecting WordPress, syncing content inventory, reading Search Console evidence, turning issues into backlog work, and approving safe SEO changes.

## Product Purpose

SEO Content Control Center helps teams find the WordPress pages costing them traffic and turn that evidence into an actionable SEO backlog. Success means an operator can understand the state of a site, identify the next useful action, and move from sync evidence to audit, backlog, preview, dry run, confirmation, and execution without guessing.

## Positioning

A review-first SEO operations cockpit for WordPress: evidence in, prioritized work out, no risky SEO write without preview, dry run, explicit confirmation, and audit trail.

## Brand Personality

Calm, exacting, operational. The interface should feel like a trustworthy command surface for focused SEO work, not a marketing page or a decorative dashboard.

## Anti-references

Avoid one long mixed dashboard where onboarding, billing, security, audit logs, synced content, and backlog all compete at the same level. Avoid oversized hero treatment, decorative card grids, vague empty states, and setup forms that obscure the current site state.

## Design Principles

- Lead with the active site state and next action.
- Separate setup, operations, administration, and account management.
- Make every risky SEO workflow visibly review-first.
- Keep dense information scannable through hierarchy, grouping, and predictable controls.
- Treat empty states as workflow guidance, not dead ends.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA for product UI: keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus states, semantic headings and tables, sufficient contrast, robust empty/error states, and reduced-motion-safe interactions.
